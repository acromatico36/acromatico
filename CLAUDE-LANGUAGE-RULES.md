# Language Rules — Words to Never Use (HARD RULE, all projects)

Italo writes clean by default and to the conscience standards of one of Jehovah's Witnesses. **Never** put the words/phrases below into any copy, content, code comment, UI text, book, email, caption, or deliverable — for any brand or client — unless it falls under "Allowed exceptions." When a draft would naturally use one, rewrite the sentence so it reads naturally without it. Write clean from the start; don't produce-then-scrub.

This is a standing rule. It applies even when a brief, template, competitor, or source text uses these words.

---

## 1. Luck / chance as a force
luck, lucky, unlucky, good luck, bad luck, beginner's luck, luck of the draw, lucky break, lucky charm, lucky stars, "thank your lucky stars", fortune, fortunate, unfortunate, fortunately, unfortunately, fortune-teller, fortune-telling, fate, fated, destiny, destined, kismet, serendipity, "meant to be", "written in the stars", "the stars aligned", "stroke of luck", "fingers crossed", "cross your fingers", "knock on wood", "third time's the charm".
**Use instead:** chance, a break, a head start, happened to, by coincidence, circumstances, your path, your future, worked out, fortunately→thankfully.

## 2. Magic / occult / supernatural
magic, magical, magically, magician, "like magic", magic bullet, magic pill, magic wand, miracle, miraculous, sorcery, witch, witchcraft, wizard, spell, charm (mystical), curse, hex, enchanted, occult, paranormal, supernatural, psychic, clairvoyant, medium (psychic), séance, ghost, haunt, demon (casual), voodoo, omen, superstition, superstitious, talisman, amulet, crystal ball, horoscope, astrology, zodiac, tarot, palm reading.
**Use instead:** quick fix, instant fix, remarkable, powerful, the real driver, "almost instantly".

## 3. New Age / manifestation / mysticism
manifest, manifesting, manifestation, law of attraction, "the universe" (as a conscious force), "the universe has your back", cosmic, karma, chakra, aura, "good/bad energy" (mystical), "send positive energy", "high vibration", vibes (mystical), zen (as belief), nirvana, "spiritual but not religious", "your truth/the universe's plan".
**Use instead:** create, build, work toward, bring about, life, the world, mindset, focus.

## 4. Deities / mythology / paganism
god, gods, goddess, the gods, deity, deities, divine, divinity, demigod, muse, titan (as deity), Zeus/Olympus/etc., "the fates", Mother Nature, Father Time, Cupid, "the man upstairs".
Also the casual exclamations: "oh my god", "OMG", "god", "thank god", "godsend", "godspeed", "god willing", "for god's sake", "act of god", "good lord", "holy grail" (as idiom), "patron saint", "guardian angel", "hallelujah", "amen" (casual), "bless / blessed / blessing" (casual), "heaven-sent", "saint" (canonization sense).
**Use instead:** rewrite plainly — "a real gift", "incredibly fast", "if all goes well", "wow", "thankfully".

## 5. Holidays/observances JWs don't celebrate (don't celebrate or theme around them)
Christmas, Easter, Halloween, Valentine's Day, Thanksgiving (as celebration), birthdays (as celebration), New Year's (as celebration), St. Patrick's, Mardi Gras, Santa, Easter Bunny, Tooth Fairy, jack-o'-lantern, trick-or-treat, mistletoe, leprechaun, "spooky/spooktacular", Cupid, resolutions-as-ritual.
**Use instead:** seasonal/neutral framing ("winter sale", "spring refresh", "year-end") without the holiday name or imagery. Don't build campaigns themed on these.

## 6. Profanity / crude / coarse (Italo does not curse)
BS, no-BS, bullsh*t, damn, hell (as expletive), crap, ass, arse, screw (vulgar), suck(s) (vulgar), piss, bastard, b*tch, "kick-ass", "badass", "WTF", freaking/frickin, "sh*t", and all stronger profanity.
**Use instead:** no-nonsense, straightforward, honest, "quick fix", "no fluff".

## 7. Also avoid: spiritism, fortune in gambling/occult framing, séance/ouija, reincarnation, "past lives", evolution-as-worldview claims framed against creation only when it's editorializing (state science neutrally).

---

## Allowed exceptions (do NOT scrub these)
- **Real proper nouns / brand & product names:** Credit Karma, Canva Magic Studio / Magic Write, Lucky Charms (if literally that product), business names, place names (e.g., "Saint" in a city/street name), book/movie titles.
- **Technical / scientific senses:** "energy" in nutrition, fitness, physics, or electricity; "spirit(s)" meaning distilled liquor; "chance/probability" in statistics; "medium" meaning size or a marketing channel; "charm" as a physics quark; "divine" only inside a quoted proper title.
- **Amazon/SEO taxonomy that is a fixed external category** (e.g., a literal "Self-Help" browse-node name) — keep the node name, but don't write the phrase into prose/marketing voice.
- **Direct quotations** where altering would misrepresent a real source — prefer choosing a different supporting quote rather than rewording someone's actual words; if unavoidable, paraphrase and label it as a paraphrase.

When unsure whether a use is the forbidden sense or an allowed technical/proper-noun sense, default to rewriting it clean.
